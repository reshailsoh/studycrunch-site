# StudyCrunch Launchpad

Build a polished, modern, responsive marketing website from scratch for StudyCrunch, an iPhone study and focus app. This is a simple public marketing site only — no authentication, database, dashboard, backend, or invented functionality.

BRAND / DESIGN DIRECTION
StudyCrunch should feel like a legitimate modern consumer iOS app for students: clean, energetic, premium, youthful, and trustworthy. Keep purple as the primary brand accent, but avoid the generic AI-startup aesthetic: no excessive glowing gradients, glassmorphism everywhere, fake metrics, fake testimonials, fake awards, fake user counts, or clutter. Use generous whitespace, strong typography, subtle motion, soft rounded cards, and a restrained purple/off-white/near-black palette. Mobile experience is extremely important.

NAVIGATION
StudyCrunch wordmark/logo on left. Links: Features, How It Works, Pricing, FAQ. Primary CTA: Download.

HERO
Eyebrow: AI LEARNING + FOCUS
Headline: “Study smarter. Lock in.”
Supporting copy: “Turn your learning material into personalized study sessions, master what matters, and block distractions when it’s time to focus.”
Primary CTA should use a clearly reserved area for Apple’s official “Download on the App Store” badge. Do not recreate or imitate Apple’s badge yourself. Include a prominent product visual area intended for a real StudyCrunch iPhone screenshot/device presentation. Do not fabricate screenshots or UI that does not exist.

FEATURE STORY
Make the page visually explain three core pillars:
1. STUDY — Users add learning material by paste/upload/photo, StudyCrunch organizes it into topics/concepts and creates personalized study questions. Study sessions support Quick (6), Full (12), and Custom modes, with topic selection.
2. MASTER — Users answer questions, receive AI-powered feedback, and track mastery/progress across topics and concepts so they can see what needs more attention.
3. FOCUS — Users choose what to study, choose a duration (15/30/45/60 minutes), select distracting apps, and StudyCrunch uses iOS Screen Time functionality to restrict those selected apps during the Focus session.

HOW IT WORKS
Create a clean visual sequence such as: Add your material → StudyCrunch organizes topics → Start a study session → Answer + get feedback → Track mastery → Lock in with Focus. Keep the copy concise and consumer-friendly.

FOCUS SECTION
Give Focus its own visually strong section because it differentiates StudyCrunch. Explain that users can restrict selected distracting apps while they work. Do not claim StudyCrunch blocks every distraction or make claims beyond the functionality described.

PRICING
Show Free and StudyCrunch Pro clearly.
Free: 1 learning goal, up to 3 materials per goal, 3 Study sessions/day, 3 Focus sessions/day, 2 successful AI Add Topic actions/day.
Pro: unlimited learning goals, materials, Study sessions, Focus sessions, and AI topic additions.
Subscription options currently displayed in the app are Monthly $4.99 and Annual $39.99. Present pricing cleanly, without inventing trials or discounts.

FAQ
Include useful questions such as: What can I upload? How does StudyCrunch create study sessions? How does Focus work? Is StudyCrunch free? What does Pro include? Keep answers limited to the functionality described here.

FINAL CTA + FOOTER
Strong final App Store download CTA. Footer should include Privacy, Terms, Support, and contact email studycrunchapp@outlook.com. Create routes/pages for /privacy, /terms, and /support, but for now make them clean placeholders that clearly preserve space for the existing approved legal/support content to be inserted verbatim later — do NOT invent replacement legal terms or privacy claims.

APPLE MARKETING CONSTRAINTS
The site will ultimately use Apple’s official App Store badge and compliant Apple product imagery. Do not create a fake App Store badge or fake iPhone hardware. Product screenshots should be placeholders until real StudyCrunch screenshots are supplied. Focus marketing on the real experience of using StudyCrunch.

TECHNICAL
Make it fast, accessible, SEO-friendly, and fully responsive. Use semantic HTML, good metadata structure, smooth anchor navigation, polished hover/focus states, and sensible reduced-motion behavior. Build the landing page so real StudyCrunch screenshots and the official App Store link/badge can be swapped in easily later.

Do not invent features, social proof, reviews, press quotes, download counts, school partnerships, or performance statistics. The goal is a simple, high-converting, premium app landing page that looks significantly more modern than a basic documentation-style website.
